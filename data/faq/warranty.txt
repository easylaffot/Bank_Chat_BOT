Warranty Policy

Manufacturer warranty
New laptops include the manufacturer's limited warranty, normally valid for one year from the delivery date unless the product listing states a different term. It generally covers defects in materials or workmanship under normal use. Accidental damage, liquid damage, unauthorized repairs, and normal wear are not covered by the standard manufacturer warranty. Coverage can vary by brand and model, so keep the product invoice and check the manufacturer's warranty terms for the exact inclusions.

Extended warranty
An optional extended warranty may be offered on eligible laptops at checkout. It extends eligible repair coverage for the term and conditions shown in the selected plan. The plan must be purchased with the laptop or during the eligibility period shown at checkout. Extended warranty does not automatically include accidental-damage protection unless the plan explicitly says so. The plan confirmation contains the provider, coverage dates, and exclusions.

How to make a warranty claim
Contact the manufacturer's authorized support center or the warranty provider listed on the invoice or plan confirmation. Provide the laptop serial number, proof of purchase, and a description of the issue. The provider may run troubleshooting steps or inspect the device before approving service. Back up personal data before handing over a laptop; service centers may erase data during repair. Keep the service request number until the claim is closed.
