Returns and Refunds

Return window
Eligible laptops can be requested for return within seven calendar days after delivery. The device must include its accessories, packaging, and proof of purchase, and must meet the condition requirements shown on the order page. Some products or promotional bundles may have different eligibility terms. Start a return from the order details page before sending anything back; an inspection may be required.

Damaged or incorrect products
If a laptop arrives visibly damaged, has missing parts, or is different from the order, report the issue through the order details page within 24 hours of delivery. Include clear photographs of the outer packaging, shipping label, and affected item. Keep the packaging and accessories until support provides instructions. Do not attempt an unauthorized repair, because it may affect return or warranty eligibility.

Refund timeline
After the returned laptop is received and passes inspection, an approved refund is initiated to the original payment method. Banks and payment providers typically take five to seven business days after initiation to post the credit; some providers may take longer. Refund timing does not include the return-shipping or inspection period. The order page shows the refund status and any adjustments for an approved partial return.
